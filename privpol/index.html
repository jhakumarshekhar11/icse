Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. This explains what ICSE Class 10 Resources (icse.shekharjha.tech), made by Shekhar Kumar Jha, collects and how it is used.

Using the site without an account

You can read and download everything without signing up. In that case your bookmarks, exam history, reading progress and theme are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and are never sent to us.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional. You can sign up with an email address and password, or with Google. We collect:

  • Your email address, and your name and profile picture if you give them or sign in with Google.
  • Your saved data: bookmarked papers, finished timed exams and the scores you entered, the last page you read in each paper, and your theme, sort order and sound settings.

We use this only to run your account: to let you log in, and to show the same bookmarks, history and settings on all your devices. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Google sign-in

If you choose Continue with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. We never receive your Google password. The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who processes your data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores accounts and saved data. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Render hosts the website files.
  • Google Analytics counts visits to the site. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • A free hit counter (Abacus) counts downloads of each paper. It receives the paper name, not your account or personal details.

The site has no advertising. It uses Google Analytics to count visits (for example how many people are on the site and which pages they open). Every visit is counted. It uses cookies, shortens your IP address, and has no advertising features. A small notice tells you about this on your first visit. To avoid it, use a browser extension that blocks analytics. The browser storage it uses (local and session storage) keeps your settings and sign-in, and is not used to track you across other sites.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. Logging out removes your bookmarks, history and progress from that browser; they stay in your account. To delete your account and all saved data, email the address below and we will delete it. You can also clear your browser data at any time to remove what is stored on your device.

Children

The site is made for school students. If you are under 18, please use an account only with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect more than the information described above.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions or deletion requests: email me. See also the Terms of Service.
