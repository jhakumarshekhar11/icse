About ICSE Resources

A free library of ICSE Class X study material in one place: previous year papers, specimen and sample papers, question banks, maps and notes. Made by Shekhar Kumar Jha, a student preparing for the same board exams.

Who made this

Hi, I'm Shekhar. I'm an ICSE student getting ready for the Class 10 board exams in 2027. I built this site so papers and study material are easy to search, read and download without hunting through folders and chats.

Outside exam prep, I'm curious about AI and machine learning, and I want to become an AI engineer one day. This site lives on the subdomain of my portfolio at shekharjha.tech, where you can see my other projects.

What's in the library

  • Previous year papers from the ICSE board exams.
  • Specimen and sample papers released for upcoming exams.
  • Question banks to practise chapter by chapter.
  • Maps and notes for Geography and English Literature.

Subjects covered: English Language, English Literature, Hindi, History & Civics, Geography, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Commercial Studies, Computer Applications and Economic Applications. New files are added over time. The New badge and the Just added row show what was added recently.

Finding what you need

  • Type what you want in the search bar, for example computer specimen 2025. The subject, type and year are picked for you.
  • Use the filter chips to choose several subjects, types or years at once.
  • Open any file to read it right in the page, or download the PDF.
  • Bookmark files with the ribbon icon and find them under Saved.

Accounts (optional)

You never need an account to read or download anything. If you create one (with an email address or your Google account), the site saves these for you and shows them on every device you log in on:

  • Bookmarks and the papers you have open, with a Continue where you left off row on the home page.
  • Timed exam history and the scores you enter.
  • Settings such as light or dark mode.

New email accounts confirm their email address first. You can change your name or password from the account menu, and ask for your account to be deleted at any time. Details are in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.

Found a mistake?

Files are uploaded by hand and errors can slip in. Use the flag icon on any resource to report it, or email me.
